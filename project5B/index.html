Part 2.6: Class-Conditioned Sampling + Remove LR Scheduler

Deliverables: sampling for 1/5/10 epochs (4 per digit) • remove scheduler and keep performance • show results + describe what changed.

I generated 4 samples per digit after 1, 5, and 10 epochs, and then retrained without the LR scheduler to keep the setup simpler while still getting similar sample quality.

Class-Conditioned Samples (1, 5, 10 epochs)
Scheduler removal (what I did)

After removing the exponential LR scheduler, I kept performance similar by using a slightly smaller constant learning rate and training the full number of epochs so the updates stayed stable.

Conclusion

Overall, time-conditioning let one model learn denoising across many noise levels, and class-conditioning made sampling converge faster and produce cleaner digits because the model knew what digit it was supposed to generate.
